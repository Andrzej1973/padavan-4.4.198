# MBSS callback integration audit

Source revision: `42d2550` (local), published target under CI: `6f78a94a`.

The callback generated by `tools/iot/prepare-mbss-checked-stop-candidate.py`
first calls `wr_iot_mbss_begin_close`, then `wr_iot_mbss_close_checked`.
Both failure paths retain the original open/module ownership. The former
can fail before a ticket exists; the latter can fail after ticket admission.

`tools/iot/mbss-recovery-submit.inc` currently queues only
`WR_MBSS_RECOVERY_OPEN_ACQUIRED`, with a NULL ticket. It cannot safely handle
all callback failures simply by being called after the checked executor.
The owner model already supports `WR_MBSS_RECOVERY_CLOSE_INCOMPLETE` and
validates the exact recorded/current ticket, closing stage and serial.

Required next implementation:

- Add submission of incomplete close with the original ticket/generation,
  preserving the same gate, netdev hold and accepted/retained result contract.
- Handle uncertain outcomes explicitly using the owner model's refusal rules;
  never reconstruct a ticket from a later radio state.
- Bind each callback failure to its corresponding ownership state. Preserve
  the original module pin until checked completion consumes it.
- Supply an adapter-pinned external caller for bounded retry and completed
  work join/reap outside RTNL and driver locks. A worker cannot reap itself.
- Verify original callback plus admission failure, partial close failure,
  successful retry, exhaustion, remove/stop collision and single ref release.

The currently published CI runs do not prove this integration: no ordinary
callback calls submission/retry yet. Runtime and final-image acceptance remain
open. This audit used repository generators/helpers and the preserved generated
candidate for comparison; it made no device access and generated no scratch.

## Uncertain-close reconciliation requirements

Inspection of `tools/iot/mbss-close-checked.inc` identifies distinct sources
that currently converge on `close_stage=5`: CR-disable failure, MBSS_Close
failure, invalid BSS index, and negative beacon DMA or TxS inventory result.
The original failed operation is not recorded in that stage value. Therefore
stage 5 alone cannot select a safe replay or establish that hardware is idle.

`prepare-mbss-cr-readback-check.py` explicitly checks control bits only:
TBTT/pre-TBTT and beacon queue disable. Its fixture does not prove RF silence,
pending RX TxS retirement, kernel synchronization or MIPS register-bus results.
Successful CR readback cannot substitute for the other uncertainty sources.

Before adding worker RECONCILE execution, retain the uncertainty origin and
last verified close stage in the exact registration/owner provenance. Require
operation-specific evidence: current exact BSS identity, producer disable,
DMA ownership and TxS callback retirement as applicable. Revalidate ticket,
generation and token after unlocked checks. Only advance to a known resumable
stage when every required receipt is available; otherwise retain ownership.
No counter decrement, module release or owner completion may follow merely
from zero pending snapshots or a successful CR readback.

## External poll caller lifetime audit

After target verification of poll (run 41660692695), inspected the actual
preparers for caller placement. prepare-mbss-work-binding.py initializes
per-slot work before recovery storage publication in common/rtmp_init.c.
prepare-mbss-pci-stop.py inserts stop/join at exactly three main recovery join
sites in rt_pci_remove: stalled, normal and post-detach. Main recovery's
separate delayed-work reaper already has synchronous cancellation, but it
does not join or own a new MBSS poll caller automatically.

Required MBSS delayed caller binding must therefore include all of:

- Fresh delayed-work initialization before adapter publication.
- A separate pending/running caller lifetime fence checked by adapter free
  paths; owner-array emptiness alone is insufficient while poll returns.
- Permanent stop publication before cancel_delayed_work_sync, outside RTNL
  and transition lock; no rearm after stop, including an empty owner array.
- Cancellation/join at all three PCI sites before worker joins and release.
- A bounded retry/rearm policy including schedule refusals, with retained
  ownership on exhaustion. A stopped caller never fabricates completion.
- Avoid making the caller's own lifetime fence cause its inner completion
  join to reject itself; distinguish inner owner/work idle from adapter-free
  eligibility.

Calling poll from an existing main reaper without these checks is not a
verified lifetime transfer. No caller binding is installed by this audit.
