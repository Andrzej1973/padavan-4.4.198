## Limits and next verification

The audit's selected-entrypoint scope is presence and shell syntax; it does
not execute lifecycle, persistence or status chains on the router. The image
gate checks compiled markers, not browser behavior or the effect of traffic
through NFQUEUE. No physical router was flashed, rebooted or modified in this
verification. Process/rule state, strategy execution, DNS/VPN and HWNAT
interaction, management access, sustained traffic, rollback and reboot
persistence remain **runtime-unverified**. The 13,064,690-byte image is not
an isolated measurement of Zapret2's compressed size contribution because
other firmware inputs changed between builds.

Result: **Zapret2 implementation is present and image-verified in #586;
device acceptance remains open.** Any device test requires separate owner
authorization and a recoverable local/VPN access path.
