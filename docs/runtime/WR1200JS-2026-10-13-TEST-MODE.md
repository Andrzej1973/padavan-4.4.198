- `uname -r`: `4.4.198`.

### Read-only ZRAM follow-up

The physical device reports `/sys/block/zram0/disksize=31657984`, `initstate=1` and a loaded `zram` module, while `/proc/swaps` remains empty apart from its header. The installed BusyBox 1.24.2 `swapon --help` exposes `-a`, `-e` and `-d`, but no `-p` priority option. Reading the installed `/sbin/rc` confirms the literal startup command `swapon -p 32767 -d /dev/zram0`. This is direct evidence of an incompatible startup argument on this installed image and a supported cause of failed swap activation; no activation attempt was made during this check.

New source/image checks already require `CONFIG_FEATURE_SWAPON_PRI=y` and confirm `-p PRI` in the shipped BusyBox help under QEMU. Those checks do not repair the currently installed BusyBox or prove activation on the router. The earlier SSH diagnostic attempt was interrupted during banner exchange; retry succeeded without a router restart.

Newer compiled images and their VPN/DNS/ZRAM UI fixes have not been installed or runtime-verified by this observation.
